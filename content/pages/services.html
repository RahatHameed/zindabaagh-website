Services

The tools and guides are free and stay free. If you want someone to go through your case with you, this is what we offer. Every service starts with a free evaluation, so you know what fits before you pay for anything.

{{block:serviceDetails}}

What we do: general information, university application support, document checklists and completeness checks, interview practice and career coaching. What we don't do: we are not lawyers and give no legal advice on your individual case under immigration or residence law, we do not file visa or residence applications for you, and we do not represent you before embassies or authorities. You submit your own applications. The German mission or immigration office decides on every application, and no one can guarantee a visa. If you need legal advice, for example after a refusal, please contact a lawyer for migration law.
