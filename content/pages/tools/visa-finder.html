How to read the result

The finder covers the main routes for study, work, job search and vocational training. It does not cover family reunion, asylum, self-employment, research stays or au pair visas. The German mission in your country decides on your application and publishes the exact document list.

You apply for a national visa (type D) at the German embassy or consulate where you live. For many countries you can now apply online through the Consular Services Portal.
