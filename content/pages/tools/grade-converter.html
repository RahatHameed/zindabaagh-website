The modified Bavarian formula

Most German universities and uni-assist convert foreign grades like this:

German grade = 1 + 3 × (best grade − your grade) ÷ (best grade − lowest passing grade)

The result is cut off after one decimal place. 1.0 to 1.5 is "very good", up to 2.5 "good", up to 3.5 "satisfactory" and up to 4.0 "sufficient".

What to keep in mind

  • The result is only as right as the pass mark you enter. A percentage with a 40% pass mark and one with a 50% pass mark convert very differently.
  • Each university decides itself how it converts, and some use their own tables. Treat this as an estimate for choosing where to apply.
  • Many master's programmes ask for 2.5 or better. Programmes with restricted admission (NC) can need much more.
