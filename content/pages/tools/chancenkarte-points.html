Step 2: points

How the points are counted

  • Qualification: 4 points if your foreign qualification is partially recognised in Germany. 1 point if it is in a shortage occupation.
  • Work experience after your qualification and related to it: 2 points for two years within the last five, 3 points for five years within the last seven.
  • German: A2 gives 1 point, B1 gives 2, B2 or higher gives 3. English at C1 or as a native speaker adds 1 point.
  • Age: 2 points if you are 35 or younger, 1 point from 36 to 40.
  • Earlier stay in Germany: 1 point. Partner applying too: 1 point.

You need at least {{facts.chancenkarte.minPoints}} points. If your qualification is already fully recognised in Germany, you get the card without the points system.

What the card allows

Up to one year in Germany to look for a job, part-time work of up to 20 hours a week, and trial work of up to two weeks per employer. When you find a qualified job, you switch to a work residence permit.

Source: Make it in Germany, Opportunity Card. You can apply online through the Consular Services Portal.
