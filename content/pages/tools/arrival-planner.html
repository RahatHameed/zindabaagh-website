Why the order matters

Almost everything depends on the address registration (Anmeldung). The registration certificate is what banks, the immigration office and many landlords ask for, and registering triggers your tax ID. So the first job is a flat or room where the landlord gives you a signed confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung).

The {{facts.anmeldungDays}}-day rule

You must register within {{facts.anmeldungDays}} days of moving in. In big cities appointments can be booked out for weeks. Book one as soon as you know your move-in date. If you cannot get one in time, keep proof that you tried: offices generally accept a late registration when the delay was theirs.
