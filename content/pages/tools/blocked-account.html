How a blocked account works

You pay in the full amount before you apply for the visa. After you arrive, the provider releases one monthly portion at a time to your normal bank account. In {{facts.year}} the required amount for students is {{facts.blockedAccount.monthly|eur}} a month. It follows the student grant rate (BAföG) and is set by the German government.

Good to know

  • Applicants for a language course or study preparation usually need 10% more.
  • Transfer a little extra. Bank charges on an international transfer can leave you a few euros short, and the confirmation is only issued for the full amount.
  • A blocked account is the most common proof, but not the only one. A scholarship or a formal commitment from someone living in Germany (Verpflichtungserklärung) can replace all or part of it.
  • The German mission in your country has a list of the providers it accepts. Check it before you open an account.

Source: Federal Foreign Office, visa service.
