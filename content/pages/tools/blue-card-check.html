Does one of these apply? Either one moves you to the lower threshold.

How the Blue Card salary rule works

The EU Blue Card is a residence permit for academics and comparable professionals with a job offer in Germany. One of its conditions is a minimum gross annual salary, which changes every January.

  • Standard threshold {{facts.year}}: {{facts.blueCard.standard|eur}} a year.
  • Lower threshold {{facts.year}}: {{facts.blueCard.reduced|eur2}} a year. It applies to shortage occupations, to people who graduated within the last three years, and to IT specialists without a degree who have at least three years of comparable experience.

What else you need

  • A concrete job offer or contract for at least six months, in a job that fits your qualification.
  • A German degree, or a foreign degree that is recognised or comparable. You can look yours up in the anabin database.
  • With the lower threshold, the Federal Employment Agency has to approve the job.
