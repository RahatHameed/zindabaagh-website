Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Applies to zindabaagh.com.

1. Who is responsible

[Full name], [Street and number, postcode, city], Germany.
Contact for all privacy questions and requests: privacy@zindabaagh.com

2. Visiting the website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads no fonts, scripts or advertising from third parties. That is why you see no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

When your browser loads a page, our web server receives the technical data every web request carries: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser and operating system. We use it only to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log files are deleted after [14] days.

3. Tools and calculators

The tools run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us. If you tick "Remember my dates and ticked tasks on this device" in the First Weeks Planner, your dates and ticked tasks are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. Untick the box or clear your browser's site data to remove them.

4. Free evaluation form

If you send the free evaluation form, we store what you enter: name, e-mail address, WhatsApp number (if given), country, your goal, education, work experience, language levels, your planned timing, your message, the page you came from and the time you sent it.

For a study visa evaluation you can also upload documents: an English certificate (for example IELTS), your CV, your degree and your transcript. They are stored in a private folder of our file storage (Cloudflare R2, EU location) and only we can open them. We use them only to assess your request. Please do not upload a passport or ID.

We use this to assess your situation, answer you by e-mail or WhatsApp, and offer you a suitable service if you want one. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to privacy@zindabaagh.com; we then delete your request.

To keep automated spam away from the form, the free evaluation page uses Cloudflare Turnstile. When the page loads, Turnstile receives technical data from your browser, such as your IP address and browser and device characteristics, to tell people from bots. Most visitors see no puzzle. Turnstile does not set cookies for advertising or tracking. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in protecting the form from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is loaded only on this page.

When a request arrives, we receive an automatic e-mail with your answers in our mailbox (Gmail), so that we can reply quickly.

We delete evaluation requests and their documents [12] months after our last contact, unless you became a client. We do not send newsletters or advertising unless you separately signed up for them.

If you ask us to reply on WhatsApp, the conversation runs through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) under its own terms. You can always choose e-mail instead.

5. Clients of our services

If you book a service (for example admission support, visa file preparation, a CV review or a call), we process the documents and details you give us to deliver that service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep invoices and related records for as long as German tax and commercial law requires (up to ten years) and delete the rest of your file [12] months after the service ends. We pass your documents to third parties, such as universities or uni-assist, only when you have asked us to submit an application for you.

6. E-mail

If you e-mail us, we use your message and address to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable format, to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to withdraw a consent at any time (Art. 7(3), 15–21 GDPR). Write to privacy@zindabaagh.com. You may also complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live or the authority responsible for us, the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
