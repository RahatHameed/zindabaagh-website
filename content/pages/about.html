Who is behind it

Zindabaagh is run by {{author}} (legal name: Rahat Hameed), who lives in Germany and went through the move personally. The site exists because the rules are public, but spread over many offices and hard to read. The tools here turn them into an answer for one situation at a time.

How the tools and guides are made

  • Official rules first. Every tool is built on the published law and government figures, and links to the source so you can check it.
  • Updated every year. Salary thresholds, tax and insurance rates change each January. The figures on this site are for {{facts.year}}.
  • Private by design. No cookies, no tracking, no account. The calculators run in your browser: nothing you type into a tool is sent to us.

What is free and what is paid

The tools and guides are free and stay free. If you want someone to go through your case with you, there are paid services. Each one starts with a free evaluation, so you know what fits before you pay for anything.

What we do not do

We are not lawyers and give no legal advice on individual cases. We do not file visa or residence applications for you and do not represent you before embassies or authorities. The German mission or immigration office decides on every application, and no one can guarantee a visa.

Found a mistake?

Rules change, and a figure can be out of date. If you spot one, please tell us through the contact form or at {{email}}, and we will correct it. Legal details are in the Impressum.
