Pathways we guide you through

Every route to Germany has its own rules, documents and timelines. Start with the one that fits you.

🎓

Study in Germany

Finding a programme, university admission and uni-assist, the student visa, the blocked account (Sperrkonto) and health insurance.

💼

Work in Germany

The EU Blue Card and skilled-worker visa, salary thresholds, job offers, and getting your foreign qualification recognised (Anerkennung).

🧭

Opportunity Card

The Chancenkarte lets qualified people come to Germany to look for work. We explain how the points system works and how to prepare.

🛠️

Ausbildung

Paid vocational training, combining a company job with classroom learning. We cover which fields are in demand and how to apply.

🗣️

Learn German

Levels A1 to C2, which certificate you need (Goethe, telc, TestDaF) and realistic study plans for each pathway.

🏠

Life after arrival

Registering your address (Anmeldung), your residence permit, tax ID, bank account, housing, and settling in with confidence.

Sample: student visa checklist

What applicants are typically asked for. Exact requirements depend on your country and the German mission handling your case, so always confirm with them.

Example only, not legal advice.

  • Valid passport
  • Admission letter from a German university
  • Proof of financial resources, often a blocked account; the required amount is set by the German government and updated regularly
  • Health insurance coverage
  • Language certificate (German or English, depending on the programme)
  • Academic certificates and transcripts
  • APS certificate (required for applicants from some countries)
  • Completed application form, photos and visa fee
